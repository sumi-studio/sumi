## Design Context

### Users

Sumi is a shared messaging and agent-collaboration workspace. People use it for ongoing chat, coordination, and direct interaction with Human and PersonalityAgent participants, often keeping the workspace open while doing other work.

### Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and responsive. The interface should feel capable without becoming visually loud, and should respect the user's attention and control.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use familiar Discord-like messaging conventions where they improve discoverability, while keeping Sumi's restrained light and dark themes. Motion should clarify state and spatial consequence rather than decorate. Avoid persistent obstruction, ornamental effects, bounce, and generic AI-styled glow or gradients.

### Design Principles

- Give every visible control a working, immediate outcome; never leave dead or misleading actions.
- Keep transient feedback brief and non-blocking, with clear manual escape hatches.
- Let interaction pause or redirect automation so the user remains in control.
- Animate only transform and opacity with restrained, directional motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve the same information hierarchy and functional access in light, dark, pointer, keyboard, and touch contexts.
