compression. It supersedes the proposed choice between selected experience and
importing current understanding from elsewhere in the context.

The compressing continuation retains the same parent context. Knowing later
experience does not authorize copying it into an earlier replacement.

For L0→L1 and L1→L2, preserve chronological placement and do not import content
from other retained L0/L1 chunks into the replacement. The selected source
chunks supply what is being compressed; they are not excluded merely because
they are currently in the upstream layer. The exclusion concerns experience
that remains outside that selected replacement. Later corrections retained
elsewhere must not become facts supposedly known at the earlier point.

L2-internal reintegration is distinct. The user emphasized chronological
preservation for operations other than L2 reintegration; this does not settle
all semantics of reintegration or authorize arbitrary mixing. Its remaining
choices should be made explicitly rather than inferred as the opposite rule.

Implementation and evaluation must distinguish full-context access from the
provenance and chronological scope of the text actually substituted. Do not
replace the full-parent fork with an isolated summarizer to enforce this rule.
This boundary does not require compulsory reflection, notes, or a personality
ritual.
